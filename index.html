Four steps between "we need blood" and "we found it."

Eliminating delays, chaotic WhatsApp forwards, and dead-end hospital phone queues.

What Blood Bank is — and isn't Blood Bank is a digital discovery, donation, and coordination platform, not a substitute for medical judgment. It helps people find verified stock and book donation slots faster; it does not reserve, sell, or medically allocate blood, and every transfusion or eligibility decision stays with licensed medical professionals and accredited blood banks. MVP scope: location-based search, verified centre profiles, stock freshness, donor booking, emergency mode, directions, and centre dashboards.
